Photo to 2D Digital Twin / Blueprint

No photo uploaded

Upload one photo of a flat object with a known reference in frame. The app produces a top-down, measurable twin and a dimensioned blueprint of the same object.

Workflow
1

Upload a photo

Angled photo, reference fully in frame, even light.

2

Calibrate

Click the reference corners. A4, or a 30 cm scale for larger objects.

3

Review and download

Check the twin against the blueprint. Download either as PNG.

Photos are held in memory on this machine only. Nothing is sent to a server.

Calibration

Place the scale flat beside the object, in the same plane, fully in frame.

Click the four corners of the A4 sheet, in order: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left.

0 of 4 points selected
